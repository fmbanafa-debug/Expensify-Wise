Your Expensify Situation

You upgraded to the Control plan — great for unlimited company card feeds!

But the built-in Expensify Card (Visa corporate card) is only available for US-registered businesses. That's why the app prompts for commercial feeds instead.

Solution path in KSA: Use your own Saudi bank corporate cards + set up commercial feeds (Visa/Mastercard/Amex) or manual/CSV imports.

Wise (Global Money App) – Great International Alternative

Wise offers a powerful app for multi-currency accounts, cheap transfers (mid-market rates), and business expense cards (physical/digital debit cards for teams with no foreign fees, spending limits, real-time tracking, 0.5% cashback).

Key for Saudi users:

  • Transfers & receiving money to/from Saudi Arabia work well.
  • Hold/convert 40+ currencies, spend in SAR like a local.
  • Business cards available for international teams, but not directly issued to Saudi residents (use if you have access via another country; digital card works globally).

Best for: International spending/transfers + expense tracking without hidden fees.
