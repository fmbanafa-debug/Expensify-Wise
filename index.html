Your Expensify Situation (Control Plan)

You upgraded to the Control plan — perfect for unlimited company card feeds!

However, the built-in Expensify Card (Visa corporate card) is only available for US-registered businesses. That's why the app asks for commercial feeds when trying to issue cards.

Recommended path in KSA:

  • Use your own Saudi bank corporate cards (Visa/Mastercard/Amex)
  • Set up commercial feeds (ask your bank to enable Visa VCF, Mastercard CDF, or Amex forms)
  • Fallback: Direct feeds (if available) or manual CSV imports

Wise Business – Strong Global/International Option

Wise offers multi-currency accounts (40+ currencies), very low-fee transfers, and business debit cards (physical/digital) with spend controls, real-time tracking & no foreign transaction fees on many purchases.

For Saudi users: Excellent for international spending & transfers. Cards work globally (including in KSA), but direct issuance for Saudi residents is limited — great as a supplement for cross-border buying.
